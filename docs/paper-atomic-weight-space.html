1. Abstract & Monolithic Null-Effect Diagnosis

Without strict decoding controls, a 0.016% sparse weight perturbation lifts monolithic benchmark accuracy from 9.28% to 48.88%. However, the unedited model scores 9.28% only because verbose reasoning chains truncate before emitting a final answer. Setting thinking=off on the unedited model produces 51.84% accuracy, whereas the sparse perturbation scores 51.60%. Three structural bottlenecks prevent monolithic weight perturbations from acquiring reliable multi-step skills. First, dense shared manifolds suffer cross-task subspace interference. Second, unconstrained solvers fit spurious topical or entity shortcuts, and open-loop error compounds exponentially across long horizons.
